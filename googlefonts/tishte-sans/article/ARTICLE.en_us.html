Tishte Sans is a screen family for interfaces and public-information systems that use Russian, Meadow Mari, Hill Mari, and Latin. Its open shapes, clear spacing, and restrained contrast remain legible at compact sizes.

The family provides Regular, Medium, SemiBold, Bold, and ExtraBold weights with matching italics. Tabular figures and stable mathematical signs make it practical for tables, dashboards, and data-heavy layouts.

Tishte Sans was developed by Sergey Yakunin with engineering support from the Tishte Project. It is derived from Arimo and distributed under the SIL Open Font License, Version 1.1.

The extended Latin and Cyrillic character set includes the letters required for both Mari literary languages, along with currency symbols, fractions, arrows, and common mathematical operators. Combining marks support decomposed Unicode input. Tabular numerals keep financial and statistical columns aligned, while balanced operator widths improve compact interface readouts.

Open counters, moderate spacing, and low-contrast strokes help text survive small sizes and varied display density. Medium and SemiBold bridge the gap between body copy and strong emphasis, giving interface designers more control than a basic four-style family without forcing artificial browser synthesis.
