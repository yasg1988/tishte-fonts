Tishte Serif is a text family designed for documents that combine Russian, Meadow Mari, Hill Mari, and Latin. It has open counters, durable serifs, and carefully positioned diacritics for the Mari letters Ӓ, Ӧ, Ӱ, Ӹ, and Ҥ.

The family provides Regular, Italic, Bold, and Bold Italic styles. Its advance widths follow a Times-compatible document model, helping existing layouts remain stable when Tishte Serif is introduced into office workflows.

Tishte Serif was developed by Sergey Yakunin with engineering support from the Tishte Project. It is derived from Tinos and distributed under the SIL Open Font License, Version 1.1.

The character set includes extended Latin, Cyrillic, punctuation, currency, fractions, arrows, and common mathematical operators. Combining marks are positioned for decomposed Unicode text as well as precomposed characters. This matters in Mari-language publishing, where text may arrive from different keyboards, document systems, and normalization workflows. Tabular numerals keep columns aligned in budgets, registers, schedules, and statistical reports.

The design uses firm stroke endings and visible internal space to remain calm in paragraphs while retaining a recognizable voice in headings. Italics are true companion drawings rather than mechanically slanted romans, and bold styles preserve the family’s proportions for predictable emphasis.
