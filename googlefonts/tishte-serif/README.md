# Tishte Serif

Tishte Serif is a document typeface for Russian, Meadow Mari, Hill Mari, and
Latin text. Its proportions remain compatible with Times New Roman for
document exchange, while its drawing and Mari-specific characters give the
family a distinct regional voice.

The family contains Regular, Italic, Bold, and Bold Italic. It is built from
the tracked SFD sources with FontForge and normalized with FontTools.

Tishte Serif is based on the OFL-licensed Tinos project. The derivative origin
and modification notice are retained as required by the SIL Open Font License.
